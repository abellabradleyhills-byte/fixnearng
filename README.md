# FixNear Nigeria

Build a mobile marketplace app called FixNear for Nigeria. It connects skilled artisans and technicians like painters, mechanics, laborers, electricians, and plumbers with customers who need their services. Two user roles: - Artisans/Technicians – must verify with NIN, BVN, and home address during registration for safety. They create profiles with skills, experience, rates, and availability. - Customers – register easily with phone number and location. Core features: - Location-based search – customers who car broke down can stay inside thier cars come online to the app and see nearby artisans within their area, like Lagos or on the road in Lokoja. Use GPS to show distance and map view. - In-app calling and messaging – contact artisans directly without leaving the app. - Emergency roadside mode – quick find for mechanics when your car breaks down, so you don’t need to step out. - Profiles with ratings, reviews, verified badge, and service history. Every artisan has a rating out of 5 stars based on customer reviews. Artisans with the highest ratings in a specific area appear first in search results and recommendations for that area. The more jobs they complete successfully, the higher their rating climbs. - Simple booking flow with date, time, and job description. Make the design clean, mobile-first, with bold colors like green and yellow for a Nigerian feel. Include safety emphasis everywhere, like verified badges and emergency help button. Start with the registration and home dashboard screens, then add search and map features
